A timeline of Libya

Ten thousand years, one coastline.

From Saharan rock art to the Garamantes, the Greek Pentapolis, Roman Africa and the present day. Drag, or use the arrows.

01
c. 8000 BCE

Prehistoric Sahara

Green-Sahara hunters and herders leave rock art across the Acacus mountains.

 02
1000 BCE – 700 CE

The Garamantes

A desert civilisation farms the Fezzan with underground irrigation tunnels.

 03
7th c. BCE

Phoenician Coast

Tyre's traders found Sabratha, Oea and Leptis — the three cities of Tripolitania.

 04
631 BCE

Greek Cyrenaica

Settlers from Thera build Cyrene and its Pentapolis above the eastern sea.

 05
96 BCE – 439 CE

Roman Africa

Leptis Magna becomes one of the empire's grandest cities under a local emperor.

 06
643 CE

Islamic Libya

Arab conquest brings Islam, trans-Saharan trade and a new architecture of light.

 07
1551

Ottoman Rule

Tripoli joins the empire; the Karamanli dynasty later rules it near-independently.

 08
1911

Italian Period

Colonial occupation and the resistance led by Omar al-Mukhtar.

 09
1951

Independence

The Kingdom of Libya is founded under Idris I; oil is discovered in 1959.

 10
1969 – today

Modern Libya

Revolution, oil and the slow, careful reopening of an extraordinary country.
